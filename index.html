What this page is

A cumulative record of automatic hybrid and naturally-aspirated petrol listings in Croatia, €7,000–15,000, 2014–2021, 60–170 tkm, under ~4.35 m. It exists to show attributes against asking price and to keep dead listings on file, not to choose a car.

Power is recorded, not required — 18 Aug 2026

The kW column is there to be read, not to filter anything out. A low figure never puts a red chip in fits brief and never keeps a car off the table.

Why it matters here: a hard 90 kW floor would have eliminated the top priority model outright. Every Yaris Hybrid on this table is 74 kW, the Yaris 1.5 petrol automatic is 82 kW, and the Jazz CVT is about 75 kW. As a column instead, it sits next to price and mileage where you can weigh it — the Auris Hybrid's 100 kW against a Yaris Hybrid that is several thousand euro cheaper.

Figures marked est. are model-typical, not read from the ad. The next run confirms them from the listings.

Criteria revised — 18 Aug 2026

Mileage narrowed to 60,000–120,000 km. Auris and Corolla now sought as hatchbacks; the Touring Sports estate is out. Engine power is now recorded in its own column but is not a requirement. Two rows were removed for breaking the fuel rule — a Ford Focus 1.0 EcoBoost, which is a turbocharged mild hybrid, and a VW Golf GTE, which is a plug-in. Their IDs are retired.

Everything else that no longer fits gets a red chip in the fits brief column and stays in the table. Rows are only ever removed when they never belonged, not when they stop being attractive — the price history is the point.

Power figures are model-typical estimates marked est., not read from the ads. The next run confirms them.

What the statuses mean

inactive means the ad is no longer on the site. It does not say why, and it is not evidence the car found a buyer — ads expire, get withdrawn, and get pulled for reasons the site never states.

unverified means the last check failed: a timeout, an error, a block. The row gets retried. It is not evidence either way.

days_listed counts from first_seen — when this tracker first saw the ad, not when it was published.

The rust edge

Rows from Dalmatia, Istria and Kvarner carry a hatched red edge on the ID cell and a coastal chip. Sea salt gets into the underbody. Rows whose region could not be read are marked coastal unknown and are not assumed inland.

Sharing

This page keeps its data in shared storage, so anyone with the link sees the same table and can edit it. Treat it as a public working document, not a private notebook.

Off-list finds

IDs starting XX- are cars that met the hard requirements but weren't on the priority list, or whose model string the scanner couldn't match. Several are far longer than 4.35 m — Mondeo at 4.87, Optima at 4.86, Q50 at 4.79, Megane Conquest at 4.51 — and stay in the record as price context rather than candidates.
